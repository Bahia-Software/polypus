`IsingObservable` (`polypus.Ising`) reported the errors of its expanded
QUBO as if they were about the caller's input. A finite field or coupling
whose expanded coefficients overflow `f64` (`-2h`, or `4J` for a coupling
near `f64::MAX`) was reported as a QUBO "linear" or "quadratic coefficient
… is not finite", and a sum above `MAX_WEIGHT_BOUND` as a bare
`Σ|c|·|scale|`, which can be up to 9 times the caller's own. The error now
names the field or coupling (or the constant they fold into) that
overflows and says the expanded QUBO cannot be built, and it reports the
bound as the expanded QUBO's sum, saying how much each field and coupling
adds to it. The error type (`ObservableError::Invalid`, a `ValueError`
from Python) is unchanged.
