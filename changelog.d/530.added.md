Measuring a sum of multi-qubit Pauli strings from counts, in
`polypus_observable::pauli`: `PauliMask` (a Pauli string as two `u64`
bitmasks, up to `MAX_QUBITS` = 64 qubits), `group_qubit_wise` (greedy
qubit-wise-commuting grouping with each group's basis rotations; identity
terms go to `Grouping::constant`), and `ParityObservable`, a
`CostObservable` for `Z` strings of any weight evaluated by bit parity, with
`expectation_from_probabilities` for exact statevector probabilities (#530).
