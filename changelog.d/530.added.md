Measuring a sum of multi-qubit Pauli strings from counts, in
`polypus_observable`: `Pauli` and `PauliMask` (a Pauli string as two `u64`
bitmasks, up to `MAX_QUBITS` = 64 qubits); `group_qubit_wise`, a greedy
qubit-wise-commuting grouping into a `Grouping` of `MeasurementGroup`s, each
with its basis rotations (`BasisRotation::H` or `BasisRotation::SdgThenH`)
and its reducer, while identity terms go to `Grouping::constant`; and
`ParityObservable`, a `CostObservable` for `Z` strings of any weight
evaluated by bit parity, with `expectation_from_probabilities` for exact
statevector probabilities. A `Σ|c|·|scale|` above `MAX_WEIGHT_BOUND` is
rejected, so no result can overflow, and counts whose total overflows `u64`
are an error (#530).
