Adjoint differentiation of the gates the parameter shift has no rule for
(#561): `polypus-sim`'s `StatevectorSimulator::adjoint_gradient` also
differentiates `Crx`, `Cry`, `Crz`, `Cp`, `Cu1`, `P`, `U1`, `U`, `UGate`,
`U2`, `Cu3` and `Cu` (each of their angles, through its generator, the phase
of `Cu`/`Cu3` relative to the control included) and `U0` (derivative `0`),
with a new reduction kernel that gives the same bits with and without the
`parallel` feature. In `polypus-qml` a model with such gates is built like
any other and gets its exact gradient, Jacobian and vector-Jacobian product
by the adjoint (`adjoint_runs`, `gradient_from_adjoint`,
`jacobian_from_adjoint`, `vjp_runs`), while the parameter-shift methods fail
with `QmlError::NoExactGradient`, which names the instruction and points at
exact mode; when exact mode cannot fit the adjoint's two statevectors it has
no shift to fall back to, and the error says so
(`QmlProblem::check_shift_fallback`, `ExpectationJacobian::check_shift_fallback`).
No layer emits these gates yet, so nothing changes for a model built from
Python. New: `AngleRef` in `polypus-circuit` (re-exported by `polypus-sim`),
the `SimError` variant `AngleIndexOutOfRange`, and `polypus-qml`'s
`test-support` feature (hidden constructors for the tests of the crates
downstream; never enabled in a library or wheel build).
