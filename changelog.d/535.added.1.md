Generic `Z₂`-symmetry tapering in `polypus-physics`:
`PauliSum::z_symmetries` finds every Z-type symmetry of a qubit
Hamiltonian, and `Tapering` turns each into a single `Z` with CNOTs and
removes that qubit, with the values `±1` of a reference basis state or
given explicitly; `Tapering::for_sector` does it for a molecule's
`FermionHamiltonian` in the Jordan–Wigner or parity encoding with the
Hartree–Fock determinant's values (H₂, LiH and BeH₂ in STO-3G go from 4,
12 and 14 qubits to 1, 8 and 9). `Tapering::apply` tapers any operator that
commutes with the symmetries, `tapered_basis_state` maps a basis state
(e.g. a VQE initial state) into the tapered space, and
`PauliSum::restricted_eigenvalues` over `Tapering::sector_basis` gives the
exact energy of an electron sector after tapering (#535).
