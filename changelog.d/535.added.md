Molecules beyond H₂ in `polypus-physics`: `chemistry::Fcidump` reads the
integrals of an FCIDUMP file (as PySCF, Molpro or Psi4 write it; restricted,
real integrals) into a `FermionHamiltonian`, with the electron sector
`(N↑, N↓)` that its `NELEC` and `MS2` give; and
`FermionHamiltonian::sector_eigenvalues` / `sector_ground_energy` compute the
exact energies of one electron sector, the FCI energy of a molecule with
given charge and spin, from a matrix of `C(K, N↑)·C(K, N↓)` determinants
(up to `MAX_SECTOR_DIMENSION` = 2500) instead of the whole `4^K` space.
Reference FCIDUMP files and FCI energies of H₂, LiH and BeH₂ in STO-3G,
generated with PySCF, are in `reference-data/fcidump-sto3g/` (#535).
