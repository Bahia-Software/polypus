An optimizer benchmark on a molecular energy: the `h2_optimizers` example of
`polypus-evaluation` trains H₂ (STO-3G, 4 qubits) along its bond-length curve
with DE, PSO, CMA-ES, SPSA, Rotosolve and Adam (with the exact
parameter-shift gradient and with finite differences) at the same budget of
circuit executions, paired seeds and an independent re-evaluation of the
final parameters, and the training guide reports the results ("Choosing an
optimizer", "A molecular energy: H₂") (#536).
