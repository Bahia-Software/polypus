For Rust users, the workspace's seeded generator is public in
`polypus_circuit::rng`: `SplitMix64` with `new`, `next_u64`, `next_f64`, the
increment `SplitMix64::GAMMA` and the finaliser `SplitMix64::mix`. The shot
sampler, the per-call seeds of contract C-7, the `polypus-qml` dataset splits
and minibatches and the probe for colliding expression checksums all draw
from this one definition, and `polypus_sim::SplitMix64` is now a re-export
of the same type. No seeded stream changes (#614).
