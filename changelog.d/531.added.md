Training a cost given as a sum of multi-qubit Pauli strings, such as a
molecular Hamiltonian, in `polypus-evaluation` (Rust; Python bindings follow
in #532): `PauliSumOracleFactory::new(template, terms)` checks a native,
measurement-free template and the `(coefficient, PauliMask)` terms, groups
them into qubit-wise-commuting groups, and builds a `PauliSumOracle` that
measures each candidate once per group (the template, the group's basis
rotations, a full measurement) with the run's shots, or `final_shots`, and
returns the energy with its sign flipped, since the optimizers maximise. A
candidate therefore costs one circuit and the run's shots per group. Invalid
input is a `PauliSumOracleError`. The design is ADR 0009 (#531).
