The `ValueError`s of `polypus.Qubo`, `polypus.Qubo.from_matrix` and
`polypus.Ising` are now in the docstrings Python shows: they were written on
the constructors, whose doc comments PyO3 does not expose, so `help()` never
listed them. They now include the coefficient bound
(`sys.float_info.max / 2**65`, about 4.9e288; checked on the expanded QUBO
for `Ising`), and the `polypus.EvaluationError` raised when one candidate's
counts add up to more than `2**64 - 1`. The training guide states the bound.
