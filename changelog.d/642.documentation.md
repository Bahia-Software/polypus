The Qiskit Aer example of `examples/python-backend-template/worker.py` passed each request's seed to `AerSimulator(seed_simulator=...)` as is, though a registered backend receives any 64-bit seed and Aer rejects one of `2**63` or more, so a provider copying it would have failed on about half of a run's requests. It now masks the seed to 63 bits (`seed & (2**63 - 1)`, keeping `None`), and the protocol description in the worker, in `worker_template.py` and in the backends guide says when to mask (#642).
