**BREAKING:** the native QML entry points of 0.8.0 refuse a complex value
instead of dropping its imaginary part: a complex `x` (`Model.fit`,
`TrainedModel.predict`/`run`, `qml.Dataset`,
`Model.circuit`/`TrainedModel.circuit`), `y` (`Model.fit`, `qml.Dataset`) or
`y_train` (`train`, `qml.train`) is a `TypeError` naming the value's
position, whatever its imaginary part and whatever holds it. A NumPy complex
array, or a NumPy complex scalar in a list, was accepted cut to its real part
with a `ComplexWarning`, while the same value as a Python `complex` was
already a `TypeError` (#602).
