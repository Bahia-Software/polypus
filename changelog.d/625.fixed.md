`QuboObservable` and `IsingObservable` (`polypus.Qubo` and `polypus.Ising`)
panicked when the shot counts of one candidate added up to more than
`u64::MAX`; they now return `ObservableError::Invalid`, as
`ParityObservable` does, and a total of exactly `u64::MAX` is still
evaluated. They also evaluated to `inf` when finite coefficients added up
past `f64::MAX` (two linear terms of `1e308`); a QUBO whose
`(|constant| + Σ|linear| + Σ|quadratic|)·|scale|` exceeds
`MAX_WEIGHT_BOUND` (about `4.9e288`) is now rejected at construction, a
`ValueError` from Python, and an Ising cost is checked on its QUBO form,
whose coefficients add up to as much as 9 times the Ising ones. Results of
every accepted cost are unchanged (#625).
