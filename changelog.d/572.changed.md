**BREAKING:** a native QML model is now bounded in size before its circuit
is built. `polypus.qml.Encoder`/`Ansatz` refuse more than 1 000 000 qubits,
and a layer that takes the model's gates past 4 000 000 is a `ValueError`
where it is added (or, for an encoder that meets more features than at
construction, the error of the call that brings the data: a
`polypus.ConfigurationError` in `fit`, `predict` and `run`). Models over
those limits that were accepted before, such as `hardware_efficient(2**40)`
or an amplitude encoder over more than 20 qubits, are now refused, by
`qml.load` too. A count too large to fit in 64 bits now reads
`layer N needs more gates than fit in a usize`. In Rust, `polypus_qml`
exports `MAX_MODEL_GATES`, `MAX_MODEL_QUBITS` and the `SizeLimit` enum, and
`ValidationError` has two new variants, `ModelTooLarge` and
`TooManyQubits`, so an exhaustive `match` on it outside its crate must
handle them (#572).
